## What to build / Requirements

To ensure your platform qualifies for the Grand Judging Panel and elite track evaluation, all teams must submit a complete, deployment-ready product portfolio. Submissions that fail to meet these premium benchmarks will be disqualified from the selection process.

 

 1. Production-Ready Deployment (Live Demo) 
The SaaS application must be fully hosted on a public cloud provider or edge network (e.g., Vercel, AWS, GCP, Fly.io).

- 
The Benchmark: The link must be live, stable, and fully accessible to the judging panel. 

 

 2. Verifiable Infrastructure (Code Repository) 
A comprehensive, public GitHub repository containing the entire codebase for the project.

- 
The Benchmark: The repository must feature a professional README.md detailing the system architecture, tech stack dependencies, database schemas, and clear instructions for local replication. Commit histories must reflect authentic collaboration.

 

 3. Operational MVP (Working Prototype) 
A functional, end-to-end Minimum Viable Product demonstrating your platform’s core value proposition.

- 
The Benchmark: The prototype must successfully execute its primary user workflows, handle authentic state management, and demonstrate structural viability under live user testing.

 

 4. Executive Briefing (Project Documentation) 
A pristine, clutter-free text breakdown detailing the business and technical anatomy of the software. You must clearly articulate:

- 
The Market Friction: The exact enterprise or consumer pain point being addressed.

- 
The Architecture: A high-level explanation of how your system solves the problem efficiently and securely.

- 
The Target Cohort: The specific user segment or demographic destined to adopt this software.

 

 5. Fiscal Architecture (Monetization Engine) 
A detailed blueprint outlining how the SaaS platform is structurally designed to capture market value and generate recurring revenue.

- 
The Benchmark: Clearly define your platform's financial framework whether driven by usage-based API billing, multi-tier subscription models (SaaS), or transactional platform fees.

 

 6. Technical Keynote (Product Demo Video) 
A high-impact, 2-to-5 minute video presentation showcasing your software in action.

- 
The Benchmark: This cinematic or high-resolution screencast must bypass fluff to immediately demonstrate the live system design, UI/UX workflow, and key feature execution.

 
⚠️ Critical Mandate: Submissions without a high-quality product demo video will be deemed incomplete and will not be eligible for grading, prize eligibility, or Devpost panel synchronization.

## Judging criteria

- 
 Technical Architecture & Scalability (20%)

 Evaluation of backend robustness, database design, and structural viability. Codebases must demonstrate production-grade engineering, optimal resource handling, and the capacity to scale seamlessly under live enterprise traffic.
 

 - 
 Enterprise Governance & Compliance (20%)

 Assessment of system security, data privacy, secure API integration, and risk mitigation. Projects must reflect structural integrity, algorithmic transparency, and responsible AI alignment for real-world deployment.
 

 - 
 Product Innovation & Market Fit (20%)

 Measuring the novelty, originality, and practical viability of the SaaS platform. The software must address a well-defined market friction or enterprise pain point with a clear, transformative execution strategy.
 

 - 
 Monetization & Fiscal Design (15%)

 Evaluation of the platform's underlying business mechanics. Teams must present a viable financial framework, such as usage-based API metering, tiered subscriptions, or transactional loops designed to capture real revenue.
 

 - 
 UI/UX & Visual Refinement (15%)

 Scoring the interface quality, user onboarding flow, and aesthetic execution. The platform must deliver a clean, high-end, and frictionless user experience that bridges advanced functionality with premium design.
 

 - 
 Keynote Pitch & Demo Completeness (10%)

 Assessment of the technical keynote presentation and the live deployed MVP. Evaluation focuses on clarity, storytelling impact, and the functional execution of core workflows demonstrated within the product demo video.
